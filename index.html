Drag any number to move it. A dashed outline marks where it started and a line connects the two. Arrow keys nudge one pixel, Shift+arrow moves ten. Copy changes puts just the moved ones on your clipboard — paste that back into the chat.
+ Annotation drops a piece of text on the map; drag it where you want it. Select one and press Delete to remove it. Annotations come out in the CSV as extra Points rows.
